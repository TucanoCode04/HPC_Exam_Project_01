SLIDE 2 -- Architecture
------------------------
On screen: text only, no image.
  Headline: "3 MPI ranks, one scenario each, OpenMP inside each"
  Small text: "Rank 0: baseline single wave -- Rank 1: two impulses at
  t=0 -- Rank 2: second impulse delayed to t_start"

Say:
"Rank 0 reproduces the single-wave baseline from assignment one. Rank 1
starts two impulses at once, to study wave interference. Rank 2 delays
its second impulse, for time-shifted interference instead. The three
ranks never exchange data -- each just runs its own full simulation loop
independently, which is what let us optimize memory allocation and
thread synchronization per rank without any cross-rank coordination
cost."

SLIDE 5 -- Splitting ranks across sockets
--------------------------------------------
On screen: combo_bars_lines_2socket.png, headline "Isolating Rank 1 on
its own socket: 88s vs 150s -- and it explains the utilization gap too".

Say:
"We moved to a partition where we could control socket placement, and
Rank 1 landed alone on one socket while the other two shared the second.
Rank 1 finished in eighty-eight seconds against one-fifty for the
others -- real evidence of socket-level memory contention. It also
explained the earlier puzzle: since MPI_Finalize is a global sync point,
whichever rank finishes first just idles waiting for the rest, and VTune
averages that idle time into its utilization score -- dragging down the
score of the fastest rank, not the slowest."

SLIDE 6 -- Full node isolation
---------------------------------
On screen: combo_bars_lines_2nodes_threads.png, headline "One dedicated
node per rank: 150s -> 88s -> 57s, best config ~29s at 48 threads".

Say:
"Giving each rank an entire dedicated node -- not just a separate socket
-- brought time down further, to about fifty-seven seconds, with Rank 0
and Rank 1 now matching almost exactly. Scaling up to forty-eight
threads per rank under full isolation got us to our best result: about
twenty-nine seconds, with CPU utilization climbing from around one
percent at a single thread to forty-eight percent at forty-eight."

SLIDE 7 -- Conclusion
------------------------
On screen: text only, 3 bullets max:
  - Real bottleneck: concurrent disk I/O, not the stencil computation
  - Socket-level memory contention is real and measurable (88s vs 150s)
  - Apparent "low utilization" was an artifact of MPI_Finalize idle time,
    not wasted compute -- caught by connecting two separate experiments

artifact, not wasted work. Best configuration: one dedicated node per
rank, forty-eight threads, about twenty-nine seconds."
