SLIDE 4 -- Nsight Systems cross-check
----------------------------------------
On screen: nsys_os_runtime_breakdown.png, headline "fclose >> fwrite --
same on A40 and V100".

Say:
"We dug deeper with Nsight Systems and found it's not even the write
call that's expensive -- it's closing the file. And we checked this on
a second, older GPU, a V100, not just our A40 -- nearly the same
pattern. So the bottleneck is I/O behavior, not something specific to
our GPU code."

SLIDE 5 -- GPU contention
---------------------------
On screen: contention_sweep_solo_vs_shared.png, headline "Solo << every
multi-rank mode -> it's disk I/O, not GPU sharing".

Say:
"That led to a direct test: give each rank its own GPU, or even its own
node, instead of sharing one. Dedicating a GPU per rank barely changed
anything. What mattered was running completely alone, with no other
rank writing at the same time. So the real cost is concurrent disk
access, not GPU sharing."

-----------------------
